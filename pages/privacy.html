1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Upaadhyay ("Upaadhyay", "we", "us", "our") runs an online faculty recruitment platform at upaadhyay.com that connects teaching and non-teaching professionals ("Faculty") with schools, junior colleges, colleges and universities ("Institutions").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, our dashboards and any service that links to this policy. We handle your information in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, as applicable.

By creating an account or using the platform you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number and password (stored in hashed form, never in plain text).
  • Faculty profile: qualifications, subjects, current role, work experience, skills, publications and research, location, preferences, profile photo, resume and certificates, and any other details you choose to add.
  • Institution profile: institution name, type, address, website, official and representative contact details, registration or affiliation numbers, logo, and sub-branch or campus details.
  • Verification documents: proof of registration or recognition, representative or employee proof, authorization letters, institution-issued ID and similar documents uploaded so that we can verify an Institution.
  • Applications and messages: jobs you apply for or post, applications, invitations, shortlists and messages sent through the platform.
  • Support requests: anything you send us through the contact form or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit statistics: on our public pages we record which page was viewed, the type of device and browser, the website that referred you, and a randomly generated visitor and session ID. We do not store your IP address in these statistics. See "Cookies and similar technologies" below.
  • Security and log data: technical logs (such as request time and error information) used to keep the service secure and working.

Please do not upload sensitive information that we do not ask for, such as government ID numbers, bank details or health information.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in securely.
  • To show Faculty profiles to Institutions, and Institution profiles and jobs to Faculty, so hiring can happen.
  • To review and verify Institutions and sub-branches before they can post jobs or search profiles.
  • To process job applications, invitations, shortlists and notifications.
  • To send service messages such as password reset, application updates, approval or rejection decisions (with the reason) and security alerts.
  • To provide support, investigate misuse and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
  • To understand how the platform is used and improve it, using aggregated visit statistics.
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your uploaded documents for advertising.

4. Consent and lawful use

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (given when you register, complete your profile, upload documents or apply for a job) and for certain legitimate uses permitted by law, such as keeping the platform secure, preventing fraud and meeting legal obligations.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the relevant information, deleting your account from Settings, or writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place, and some features may stop working without the information they need.

5. Who can see your information

Faculty. Your profile (such as name, qualifications, experience, skills and location) is visible to approved Institutions so they can find and shortlist you. Your resume and certificates are private files: they are opened only through short-lived, signed links and are shared with an Institution when you apply to its job or when you choose to share them. Your password is never visible to anyone.

Institutions. Your institution name, type, location, logo and job postings are visible to Faculty. Your verification documents are visible only to Upaadhyay administrators and are used solely for review.

Service providers. We use trusted providers to run the platform, such as web hosting, cloud file storage, email delivery and security services. They may process data only on our instructions and under confidentiality obligations.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or Upaadhyay.

Business changes. If Upaadhyay is merged, acquired or restructured, your information may move to the new owner, who must continue to protect it under this policy.

6. Where your data is stored and how we protect it

Account and profile data is stored in a database on our hosting provider. Uploaded files such as photos, logos, resumes, certificates and verification documents are stored in private cloud storage. Some of our service providers may operate servers outside India; where this happens, we expect them to protect your data to a standard comparable to this policy.

We use reasonable security measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access tokens, role-based access so only authorised administrators can view Institution documents, signed and time-limited file links, and rate limiting against abuse. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password confidential and use a strong, unique password.

If we become aware of a personal data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers. Our site uses your browser's storage to keep things working:

  • Login session: a secure access token is kept in your browser so you stay signed in until you log out or it expires.
  • Visit statistics: a random visitor ID (kept in local storage) and a session ID (kept for the browsing session) let us count visits and "online now" in a privacy-friendly way. They are not linked to your name or email on public pages, and we do not store IP addresses in these statistics.
  • Preferences: small settings such as your chosen language.

Our visit counter respects your browser's Do Not Track setting and is switched off in the admin area. You can also clear your browser's site data at any time. For the full list of what we store and why, see our Cookie Policy.

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. When you delete your account from Settings, we delete your profile and remove your uploaded files (photos, logos, resumes and documents) from storage. We may keep limited records for a reasonable period where needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce our terms. Aggregated visit statistics older than about 180 days are deleted automatically.

Messages and applications that you sent to others may remain visible to the other person.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete information (most of it can be edited directly in your profile);
  • Erase your data by deleting your account in Settings, or by asking us to do it;
  • Withdraw consent to processing;
  • Grievance redressal if you are unhappy with how your data is handled; and
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, as the law provides.

To exercise a right, write to support@upaadhyay.com from the email address registered on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational, or any other authority entitled to hear such complaints.

10. Children

The platform is meant for professionals and Institutions and is not intended for anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

Job pages, Institution profiles and messages may link to websites that we do not control. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, so please read their policies before sharing information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make a material change we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email or on the platform. Continued use of the platform after a change means you accept the updated policy.
